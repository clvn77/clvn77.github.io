Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

MultiTimer: Tap & Go is provided by Denis Butakov. For privacy questions and requests, contact safir.app.support@gmail.com.

Current test releases use Google sample advertisements. Production advertising is not enabled. Version 0.7.0 introduces the uniform advertising approach described below; earlier test versions used an age selection and an advertising-free child mode.

Information stored on your device

Your timer durations, names, artwork, colors, sound choices, running timer state and app preferences are stored locally. You do not need an app account. We do not operate an application server that receives your timer settings.

If you choose an audio file or a system alarm sound, the app saves a copy in its private storage for later playback. On iOS, imported audio is converted to a notification sound using up to the first 29 seconds. The app does not upload your audio files or timer names to our server or intentionally include them in advertising requests.

Alarms and permissions

The app uses operating-system alarms and notifications to alert you when a timer finishes. On Android it also displays running countdown notifications. Timer names can appear on the lock screen and in notifications; choose a name you are comfortable displaying there. You can control notification visibility and permissions in device settings. The audio picker gives access to the file you select; the app does not require access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.

Advertising and younger users

Starting with version 0.7.3, the app asks once for an age group: Under 18, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say. The answer is stored only on your device and can be changed in Settings. We do not request a date of birth or send your selected group to our server. Choosing a younger or unspecified age does not remove advertising; there is no free child-mode advertising switch. Advertising requests use the same restrictive configuration for everyone: child age treatment, a maximum Google ad content rating of G and non-personalized ad requests. The app does not use your timer name or chosen sound to personalize advertisements.

These settings restrict ad delivery and processing; they do not mean that advertising involves no data collection. Google's SDK may process IP addresses, device or SDK identifiers, diagnostics and interactions with advertisements for delivery, measurement, reliability and fraud prevention. The integration removes Android advertising-ID permissions and does not request Apple's tracking authorization; other SDK identifiers may still be used.

The home screen can display a banner. A dismissible advertising break may appear when entering customization, subject to availability and frequency limits. Advertising is not shown over a completion alarm, and an unavailable advertisement does not prevent using the timers.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required and available, Settings offers Privacy choices for Google's privacy controls. Third-party SDKs may continue processing work already started during the current app session.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to remove banner advertisements and advertising breaks. It is not a subscription. Availability and the displayed local price are provided by the App Store or Google Play. Payment processing and purchase history are managed by the store; we do not receive your payment-card details.

The app processes product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and store verification information to validate ownership and restore purchases. Android stores signed purchase verification information locally and checks store ownership when the store is available. iOS checks verified StoreKit entitlements. No purchase-verification application server is operated by us. Removing local app data does not cancel store ownership; use Restore purchases with the original store account.

Your choices and deletion

Use device settings to manage alarms, notifications and lock-screen information. Privacy choices is available when required by the advertising service. Timers remain usable when advertising or the store is unavailable.

You can remove app-managed settings, timer names and imported audio copies by clearing app data or uninstalling the app according to your operating system's behaviour. See Your data for instructions. This does not automatically delete data already received by Google or your store provider; their policies explain retention and controls.

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request or investigate the issue. Avoid sending information that is not necessary. Contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you supplied to support.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to this website. GitHub may process technical request information as the hosting provider; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy updates

We will update this policy as app features or provider behaviour change. The page will show the effective date, and material changes will be communicated as appropriate. Before enabling live advertising, we will review this policy against the shipping integration.
