Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

MultiTimer: Tap & Go is provided by Denis Butakov. For privacy questions and requests, contact safir.app.support@gmail.com. This inbox also supports Safir; please include “MultiTimer” in your message.

The current store testing build, 0.6.3 (9), does not contain advertising. Advertising integration is under development and disabled by default. The advertising sections below describe builds in which that feature is enabled; they do not mean that live advertising is already active.

Timer settings on your device

The app stores timer durations, artwork, sounds, background preferences and, in advertising-capable builds, your selected age group locally on your device. You do not need an app account. We do not operate an application server that receives your timer settings or age choice.

The age choice is a broad group, not a date of birth. It controls whether advertising is available. We do not intentionally send your timer configurations or age selection to advertising providers.

Alarms and permissions

The app uses operating-system alarms and notifications to alert you when a timer finishes. You can change permissions in your device settings. The app does not require access to your contacts, microphone, or precise location.

Advertising-enabled builds

These builds use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. The app requests advertising only for users who select “18 or older.” It does not request ads for users who select “Under 18” or “Prefer not to say.” Applicable consent choices are presented before requesting ads where required.

Adult advertising requests are configured as non-personalized and restricted to a maximum general-audience content rating. Non-personalized advertising still involves processing information.

Google's advertising components may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app or device identifiers, diagnostics, and advertising interactions. These can support ad delivery, measurement, reliability, and fraud prevention. The integration removes Android advertising-ID permissions and does not request Apple's tracking authorization; other SDK identifiers may still be used.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for its processing and privacy controls.

Children and shared devices

Timers remain available without advertising for users under 18 and people who do not specify their age. Before sharing a phone with a child, use “A child will use this device” in Settings. This removes the banner and stops new advertising requests from the app.

If the advertising SDK already started in an adult session, close and reopen the app before handing the phone to a child. Changing a setting cannot undo data already processed or cancel all work already started inside a third-party SDK. The integration has not yet completed native-device privacy verification and live advertising remains disabled by default.

Your choices and deletion

In builds with age controls, you can change the age group or enable child mode in Settings. Where required and available, “Privacy choices” opens Google's advertising consent options. Timers work when advertising is unavailable.

You can remove app-managed local settings by clearing app data or uninstalling the app according to your operating system's behaviour. See Your data for instructions. This does not automatically delete data already received by Google; Google's policies explain its retention and controls.

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request or investigate the issue. Avoid sending information that is not necessary. You can contact us about access, correction, or deletion of information you supplied to support.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to this website. GitHub may process technical request information as the hosting provider; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy updates

We will update this policy as app features or provider behaviour change. The page will show the effective date, and material changes will be communicated as appropriate. Before enabling live advertising, we will review this policy against the shipping integration.
