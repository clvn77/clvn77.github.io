Your data

MultiTimer does not require an app account or maintain a cloud account containing your timer settings.

Remove settings from your device

  • Android: open device Settings → Apps → MultiTimer → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names vary by device. You can also uninstall the app.
  • iPhone: open Settings → General → iPhone Storage → MultiTimer → Delete App. “Offload App” keeps documents and data, so use Delete App when you want to remove them.

Removing local data resets your timers and preferences. Device backups are controlled separately by your operating system and account settings.

Advertising and support information

Removing the app does not automatically delete information already processed by an advertising provider. See the Privacy Policy for provider links and available choices.

For questions or a request concerning information you sent to support, email safir.app.support@gmail.com with “MultiTimer data request” in the subject. Tell us what you would like us to review. Do not send identity documents unless we separately explain why they are necessary.
