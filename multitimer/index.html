How can we help?

Support for MultiTimer: Tap & Go, a timer app for Android and iPhone with four independent timers, playful artwork and completion alerts.

safir.app.support@gmail.com

We use one shared support inbox for MultiTimer and Safir. Please put MultiTimer in the subject so we can identify your app.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. For alarm issues, tell us whether the app was open, in the background, or the screen was locked.

Screenshots can help. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or personal information that is not needed to explain the issue.

Privacy and family use

Read the Privacy Policy to learn about timer settings, age choices and advertising-enabled builds. For local deletion instructions, see Your data.

Support requests sent by email are used to respond to your question and investigate the reported issue.
